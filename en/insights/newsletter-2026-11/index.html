03 · Featured topic

Tax benefits decided before 31 December

For several tax benefits, what is done and documented by the year-end weighs directly on 2026 corporate income tax (IRC). Some have application deadlines already in 2027.

SIFIDE II

Extended to periods up to 2026 (Decree-Law 170/2026). Base rate of 32.5% of eligible expenses, plus 50% of the increase over the average of the two preceding financial years, with a limit of €1.5 million for the incremental component, where applicable. Application for 2026 by 31 May 2027.

RFAI

Deduction from the corporate income tax payable on relevant applications in eligible regions and sectors: in the regions of Article 107(3)(a) of the TFEU (Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union), 30% up to €15 million of investment and 10% on the excess; in the regions of point (c), 10%. The limits on the deduction from tax payable and the regional-aid limits of the 2022–2027 map also apply. Investment carried out in the period counts. Mere advances do not count as additions to investments in progress; their later completion requires analysis of the rules in Article 22 of the Investment Tax Code (CFI). A tax documentation file is required, and the assets must be retained.

Incentive to the capitalisation of companies (ICE)

Deduction from taxable profit calculated on the eligible net increases in equity of the period and of the six preceding periods, taking into account only periods beginning on or after 1 January 2023, up to the higher of two limits: €4 million or 30% of tax EBITDA. Rate: the period's average 12-month Euribor, plus 2 percentage points. In 2026 the deduction benefits from a transitional uplift of 20%, subject to the statutory limit.

Salary increase incentive

Eligible costs of pay increases taken into account at 200%, provided both minimum-increase tests of 4.6% are met: the company's average annual base pay, and the annual base pay of workers earning up to the company average at the end of the previous year, together with the other legal conditions.

IFR (2022 balance)

Possible use of an IFR (tax incentive for recovery) balance relating to eligible investment of 2022, subject to the available balance, the carry-forward period and the other conditions.

Before the year-end, it is worth confirming eligibility, gathering the documentation and simulating the effect on the 2026 tax.

04 · Current developments

Alerts and news

2027 State Budget — a proposal, not yet approved

The proposal was approved in general on 28 October. The parliamentary calendar provides for the deadline for tabling amendments to end on 6 November, voting in detail to begin on 18 November and the final overall vote to take place on 24 November. Among the proposed measures: a 4.5% threshold for the salary increase incentive and the extension of SIFIDE II to 2027. The measures indicated are proposals and depend on the final text as approved, on enactment and on publication.

Corporate income tax: the reduction is already law

Law no. 64/2025 set the standard corporate income tax rate at 19% for periods beginning in 2026, 18% for periods beginning in 2027 and 17% for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2028. For SMEs (small and medium-sized enterprises) and Small Mid Cap entities that meet the requirements, 15% on the first €50,000 of taxable income, from periods beginning in 2026.

Social Security: new return model

Under the new model (Decree-Law 127/2025), Social Security calculates the remuneration and the contributions, and the employer confirms or corrects the amounts by the 20th of the following month. Payment is made between the 1st and the 25th. The transition runs through 2026; the new model covers all employers from 1 January 2027. Failure to respond within the deadline is equivalent to acceptance of the amounts calculated.

05 · Planning ahead

December deadlines, in advance

  • 7 Dec

    Reporting of the invoice data for November (e-fatura).

  • 10 Dec

    Monthly remuneration return to the AT and, for entities not yet covered by the new model, the remuneration return to Social Security, both for November.

  • 15 Dec

    For tax periods coinciding with the calendar year, the third PPC (payment on account) and the third additional payment on account fall due on 15 December. As to the PPC, Article 107 of the CIRC (Corporate Income Tax Code) allows the third instalment to be dispensed with if the payments already made cover the estimated tax, or limited to the difference when the normal instalment exceeds that difference. An insufficient estimate may give rise to compensatory interest.

  • 21 Dec

    Monthly VAT (October), the recapitulative statement and the DMIS; payment of withholding tax and stamp duty; personal income tax (IRS): third payment on account.

  • 21 Dec

    Payment of the November contributions of self-employed workers (the 20th is a Sunday).

  • 28 Dec

    Payment of VAT, monthly scheme.

  • 28 Dec

    Payment of the employers' contributions and the employees' contributions for November (the 25th is a public holiday, followed by a weekend).

  • 31 Dec

    Modelo 30 (income paid or made available to non-residents in October) and IUC for vehicles whose registration anniversary falls in December, without prejudice to the applicable special regimes.

To prepare for the close · Inventories

Prepare the reporting of 2026 inventories: the general deadline is 1 February 2027, for entities whose financial year coincides with the calendar year and that are subject to the obligation.
