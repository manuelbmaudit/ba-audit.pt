Newsletter privacy notice

Controller and contact. Manuel Borges, Revisor Oficial de Contas (Statutory Auditor), registered with the Ordem dos Revisores Oficiais de Contas (OROC) under no. 1067, practising as BA AUDIT, Rua São Salvador da Baía, 7, 1D, 2800-201 Almada, Portugal. For data protection enquiries, contact manuel.borges@ba-audit.pt. No data protection officer has been appointed, as this is not mandatory for this processing.

Data, purposes and legal basis. We use your email address and, if you provide it, your name to send you BA AUDIT’s monthly newsletter and occasional information on audit, accounting and tax. We also process subscription records, including the date, time and IP address, to record and demonstrate your consent. Sending these communications is based on your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Article 13-A(1) of Portuguese Law no. 41/2004. You must select the consent box and confirm your subscription using the link sent by email. Only people who subscribe through the form and confirm are added to the mailing list. A professional relationship does not result in automatic subscription.

Voluntary subscription. Providing your email address is voluntary and is not a statutory or contractual requirement. Without it, we cannot send you these communications. Providing your name is optional. Your subscription will not become active without your consent and email confirmation. We do not use these data for automated decision-making or profiling.

Service provider and recipients. Brevo SAS, 9–17 rue Salneuve, 75017 Paris, France, processes data on our behalf to provide the email platform, under an Article 28 GDPR processing agreement. Data are hosted on servers in the European Union. We do not disclose your data to third parties for their own purposes.

International transfers. Some of Brevo’s network, security and technical support providers may access data from outside the European Economic Area, including the United States and India. Where applicable, transfers rely on European Commission standard contractual clauses with supplementary measures, or, for eligible US recipients, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, under Articles 45 and 46 GDPR. You may request further information and a copy of the applicable safeguards at manuel.borges@ba-audit.pt.

Measurement. Our emails include a small image (pixel) and redirected links that allow opens and clicks to be counted. In the reports available to us, these events are shown in aggregate and are not associated with your email address or IP address; we do not use this information to identify who opened an email or clicked a link.

Retention. Subscription data are kept while your subscription is active. After you unsubscribe, we remove your data from the mailing list and retain only: (i) your email address on a suppression list for as long as necessary to respect your cancellation and prevent further unsolicited communications; and (ii) the consent record, including the date, time, IP address, form and wording accepted, and email confirmation, for three years after cancellation, to demonstrate the lawfulness of the communications. The consent record is then deleted. The suppression list is used only to respect cancellations, and the need to retain it is reviewed periodically.

Your rights. You may withdraw your consent at any time using the unsubscribe link in each email or by contacting manuel.borges@ba-audit.pt. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Subject to the applicable conditions, you may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection under Articles 15–21 GDPR by contacting the same address. You may lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), Av. D. Carlos I, 134, 1.º, 1200-651 Lisboa, Portugal, www.cnpd.pt.

Related policy. The general website Privacy Policy is currently available in Portuguese: Privacy Policy (in Portuguese). This notice provides the information in English about the newsletter subscription.
