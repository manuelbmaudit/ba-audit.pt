03 · Featured topic

Tax benefits decided before 31 December

For several tax benefits, what is done and documented by the financial year-end has a direct bearing on 2026 IRC. Some have application deadlines in 2027.

Sistema de Incentivos Fiscais em Investigação e Desenvolvimento Empresarial II (SIFIDE II — System of Tax Incentives for Business R&D)

Extended to periods up to 2026 (DL – Decree-Law 170/2026). Base rate of 32.5% of eligible expenditure, plus 50% of the increase over the average of the two previous financial years, capped at €1.5 million for the incremental component, where applicable. Application for 2026 by 31 May 2027.

Regime Fiscal de Apoio ao Investimento (RFAI — Investment Support Tax Scheme)

Deduction from IRC payable on relevant investments in eligible regions and sectors: in the regions referred to in Article 107(3)(a) TFEU (Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union), 30% on investment up to €15 million and 10% on the excess; in the regions referred to in point (c), 10%. The limits on deductions from tax payable and the regional aid limits of the 2022–2027 regional aid map also apply. Investment made during the period counts. Mere advance payments do not count as additions to investments in progress; their subsequent completion requires analysis under the rules of Article 22 of the CFI (Investment Tax Code). A tax documentation file and retention of the assets are required.

Incentivo à Capitalização das Empresas (ICE — Incentive to the Capitalisation of Companies)

Deduction from taxable profit calculated on the eligible net increases in equity in the financial year and the six preceding years, considering only periods beginning on or after 1 January 2023, up to the higher of two limits: €4 million or 30% of tax EBITDA. Rate: average 12-month Euribor for the period, plus 2 percentage points. In 2026, the deduction benefits from a transitional 20% uplift, subject to the legal limit.

Salary increase incentive (valorização salarial)

Eligible costs relating to salary increases are taken into account at 200% of their amount when determining taxable profit for IRC purposes, provided both minimum-increase tests of 4.6% are met: an increase in the company's average annual base pay and an increase in the annual base pay of employees whose annual base pay at the end of the previous year was no higher than the company average, subject to the other statutory conditions.

Incentivo Fiscal à Recuperação (IFR — Tax Incentive for Recovery): unused 2022 tax credit

Possible use of an unused IFR tax credit relating to eligible 2022 investment, subject to the available balance, the carry-forward period and the other applicable conditions.

Before the year-end, it is advisable to confirm eligibility, gather the documentation and simulate the effect on 2026 tax.

04 · Current affairs

Alerts and updates

2027 State Budget — proposal, not yet finally approved

The general principles of the proposal were approved on 28 October. The parliamentary calendar provides for a deadline of 6 November for tabling amendments, voting on the details to begin on 18 November and the final overall vote to take place on 24 November. Among the proposed measures: a 4.5% threshold for the salary increase incentive and the extension of SIFIDE II to 2027. The measures indicated are proposals and depend on the final approved text, promulgation and publication.

IRC: the reduction is already law

Law no. 64/2025 set the general IRC rate at 19% for periods beginning in 2026, 18% for periods beginning in 2027 and 17% for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2028. For PME (SMEs) and Small Mid Caps meeting the requirements, 15% on the first €50,000 of taxable income, from periods beginning in 2026.

Segurança Social: new return model

Under the new model (DL 127/2025), the Segurança Social calculates remuneration and contributions, and the employer confirms or corrects the amounts by the 20th of the following month. Payment is made between the 1st and the 25th. The transition takes place during 2026; the new model covers all employers from 1 January 2027. Failure to respond within the deadline is treated as acceptance of the calculated amounts.

05 · Planning ahead

December deadlines: a look ahead

  • 7 Dec

    Reporting of November invoice data (e-fatura).

  • 10 Dec

    DMR to the AT and, for entities not yet covered by the new model, Declaração de Remunerações to the Segurança Social, for November.

  • 15 Dec

    For tax periods coinciding with the calendar year, the 3rd PPC (pagamento por conta – payment on account) and the 3rd additional payment on account fall due on 15 December. As regards the PPC, Article 107 of the CIRC (Corporate Income Tax Code) allows the third instalment to be waived if the payments already made cover the estimated tax, or limited to the difference where the normal instalment exceeds that difference. An insufficient estimate may give rise to compensatory interest.

  • 21 Dec

    Monthly IVA (October), recapitulative statement and DMIS; payment of withholding taxes and Imposto do Selo; IRS (Personal Income Tax): 3rd payment on account.

  • 21 Dec

    Payment of November contributions by self-employed workers (the 20th is a Sunday).

  • 28 Dec

    Payment of IVA, monthly scheme.

  • 28 Dec

    Payment of employer and employee contributions for November (the 25th is a public holiday, followed by a weekend).

  • 31 Dec

    Modelo 30 (income paid or made available to non-residents in October) and IUC for vehicles whose registration anniversary falls in December, without prejudice to the applicable special regimes.

Preparing for year-end · Inventories

Prepare the 2026 inventory submission to the Portuguese Tax and Customs Authority (AT): general deadline 1 February 2027, for entities whose financial year coincides with the calendar year and that are subject to the reporting obligation.
